# NOMI MOBILES — Native Android App

An offline-first, single-device mobile shop management application built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and a local Room database. Designed specifically for shopkeepers and mobile store owners selling iPhones and Android handsets in Pakistan.

---

## Features

- **Dashboard**: Live calculated financial overview (Stock Count, iPhones vs Android breakdown, Stock Valuation, Current Month Sales, Expenses, and Net Profit) alongside an interactive Canvas bar chart (Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 12 Months).
- **Inventory Management**: Search and filter by category (iPhone / Android) and status (In Stock, Sold, Returned). Detailed handset fields: Category, Brand, Model, SKU, IMEI 1 & IMEI 2, PTA Status (PTA Approved, Non-PTA, Non-PTA SIM Working, JV, Bypass), Storage, RAM, Battery Health (50–100%), Condition, Accessories, Cost & Asking Price.
- **Sales & Customer Dues**: Create sales directly from in-stock phones. Supports full and partial down-payments, computes remaining balances in real time, generates printable & shareable receipts, and records subsequent customer debt collections.
- **Returns & Warranty**: Return sold phones back to stock. Calculates net retained receipts and profit automatically.
- **Purchases & Supplier Accounts**: Register handset purchases with automatic stock creation in a single atomic Room transaction. Track supplier debts and allocate payments using FIFO (First-In, First-Out).
- **Customers & Suppliers**: Standalone contact directories showing order counts and outstanding balances.
- **Shop Operating Expenses**: Categorize rent, utility bills, salaries, tea/refreshments, and repairs to calculate true net profit.
- **Financial Reports & P&L**: Daily, 7-day, 30-day, Year-to-Date and All-Time reports. Explains exact revenue, gross margin, operating expense, and net profit calculations in PKR (Pakistani Rupees).
- **Local Backup & Restore**: Export complete database to a single JSON file via Android Storage Access Framework; restore with safe merge or replace choices. Also exports CSV spreadsheets for Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
- **Theme**: Signature Dark Navy & Cyan/Teal theme with toggle for Light mode.

---

## Non-Negotiable Local-Only Storage

- **100% Offline**: The app does **not** declare `<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />`.
- **Room Database**: Persists all entities (`phones`, `sales`, `sale_payments`, `sale_returns`, `purchases`, `supplier_payments`, `customers`, `suppliers`, `expenses`) locally on the device in SQLite via Room.
- **DataStore**: Stores shop profile (shop name, phone, address, receipt footer) and theme preference.

## Build & Run Prerequisites

1. **Android Studio**: Ladybug / Hedgehog or newer.
2. **JDK**: Java 17 or Java 21 configured in Android Studio Gradle settings.
3. **Android SDK**: `minSdk 24` (Android 7.0), `compileSdk 36`, `targetSdk 36`.

### Building via Terminal
```bash
gradle assembleDebug
```

## Manual QA Verification Checklist

1. **First Launch Empty State**: Open app, verify all metrics start at zero with no fake pre-seeded data.
2. **Adding Mobile to Stock**: Tap **Add Mobile** from Dashboard or Inventory. Enter model, brand, SKU, purchase cost, asking price, PTA status, and optional 15-digit IMEI. Save and verify it appears immediately under In Stock.
3. **Duplicate Active IMEI Prevention**: Attempt adding a second handset with the same active IMEI; verify inline error prevents duplicate inventory entry.
4. **Recording a Sale with Partial Payment**: In Inventory, tap **Sell** on an in-stock handset. Enter customer name, set partial payment (e.g. Paid Rs 200,000 of Rs 250,000). Confirm sale.
5. **Receipt Generation & Sharing**: After sale, verify the receipt dialog displays shop name, invoice number, phone description, IMEI, paid amount, and remaining due of Rs 50,000. Tap **Share Receipt** to verify standard Android Intent export.
6. **Receiving Customer Due**: In Sales, find the partial sale. Tap **Receive Due**, record the remaining balance, and verify remaining due drops to Rs 0 and badge updates to "Paid".
7. **Processing Return**: On a completed sale, tap **Return**, enter refund amount, and confirm. Verify phone status is restored to "In Stock" in inventory and sale is marked "Returned".
8. **Recording Purchase & Supplier Dues**: Tap **New Purchase**, enter phone and supplier details, pay a partial amount. Verify phone is added to stock and purchase record displays outstanding balance. Use **Pay Supplier** to test FIFO debt settlement.
9. **Operating Expenses & Profit Calculation**: Add an expense (e.g. Rent Rs 45,000). Check Dashboard and Financial Reports to verify Net Profit = Gross Profit - Operating Expenses.
10. **JSON Backup & Safe Restore**: Go to **More → Settings**, tap **Export Backup**, save the `.json` file. Then load sample demo data or reset, and tap **Restore Backup** to verify clean merge/replace without data loss.
11. **App Restart Persistence**: Force-close and reopen the app; verify all records remain intact in Room SQLite storage.
